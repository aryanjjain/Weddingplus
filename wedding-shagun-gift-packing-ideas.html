Wedding Shagun & Gift Packing Ideas for 2026

Beautiful ways to present shagun and gifts at every Indian wedding function

Shagun packing is one of the most cherished traditions in Indian weddings. Whether it's cash, dry fruits, sweets, or jewelry, how you present a shagun matters just as much as the gift itself. At Wedding Plus, Jalandhar's trusted wedding cards and boxes store, we've helped hundreds of families turn simple gifts into memorable presentations. Here are our favorite shagun and gift packing ideas for 2026.

1. Decorative Shagun Envelopes

Gone are the days of plain paper envelopes. Modern shagun envelopes now feature hand-embroidery, zari borders, velvet finishes, and even personalized names in gold foil. These small but elegant touches make cash gifts feel far more special.

2. Themed Shagun Trays

Shagun trays are a beautiful way to present multiple items together — cash, coconut, dry fruits, and sweets — arranged on a single decorated platter. Popular themes for 2026 include:

  • Royal Rajwada — deep maroon and gold with mirror work
  • Floral Pastel — soft pinks and greens with fresh or artificial flowers
  • Minimal Boho — jute, cane, and dried flower accents

3. Mini Potli Bags for Dry Fruits & Mithai

Small fabric potli bags in silk or tissue material are perfect for gifting dry fruits or sweets alongside the main shagun. They're reusable, easy to customize with tassels or beadwork, and always look festive on a gift tray.

4. Coordinated Packing with Wedding Cards

A trend we love at Wedding Plus is matching your shagun packing with your wedding invitation card design — same color palette, same motifs. It creates a cohesive, premium look across every part of the celebration, from the invite to the ring ceremony tray.

5. Eco-Friendly Packing Options

More couples are choosing sustainable materials — jute pouches, seed-paper envelopes, and reusable wooden boxes — for their shagun and gift packing. It's a thoughtful way to celebrate while being kind to the environment.

Tips for Getting It Right

  • Match the packing style to the specific function — engagement, sangeet, or wedding day each has its own vibe.
  • Don't overcrowd a single tray; let each item have its own space.
  • Add a small personalized tag or card for a thoughtful finishing touch.
